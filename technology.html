Supply Chain Intelligence Engine.

An integrated analytical platform being developed by Chain Genius Consulting to connect operational data with explainable supply-chain insight.

One platform. Connected intelligence.

The engine is designed to bring multiple areas of supply-chain analysis together, helping businesses understand how inventory, suppliers, demand, purchasing and financial exposure relate to one another.

It is being developed around repeatable calculations, transparent assumptions, data validation and evidence-backed findings.

Designed around five principles

◎

Explainability

Findings should be traceable to source data, methods and assumptions.

✓

Data integrity

Customer source data should not be silently changed.

↔

Consistency

Connected modules should rely on shared analytical foundations.

⌁

Honesty

Estimates, simulations and measured outcomes should be clearly distinguished.

▤

Provenance

Methods, inputs and calculation basis should be understandable.
