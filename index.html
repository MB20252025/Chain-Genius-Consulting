About Chain Genius

Built around the realities of running a supply chain.

Supply chain decisions are connected. A purchasing issue can affect production; a planning change can alter stock requirements; and a supplier delay can have consequences across the business.

Chain Genius Consulting is focused on making those connections easier to see. We combine practical operational understanding with straightforward analysis and purpose-built digital tools.
