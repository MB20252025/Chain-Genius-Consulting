 This compares the workbook’s recorded historical forecasts with actual sales. It is separate from the Demand forecast tab, which builds new forecasts. Three things are kept apart: forecast performance (how close the forecasts were), data quality (whether the history is complete enough to judge), and confidence in the information (how far the assessment can be trusted, not whether the forecast itself is good). Strong accuracy on thin or unusually consistent data is not reported as excellent forecasting.
