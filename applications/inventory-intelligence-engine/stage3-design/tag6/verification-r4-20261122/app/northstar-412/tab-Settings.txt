 Fixed rules (not configurable)Seasonal forecasts need 24 months, because seasonal indices cannot be estimated from less than two annual cycles. The MAD scale factor 1.4826 makes the robust z-score comparable to a standard deviation. Two or more consecutive extreme months are treated as a level shift, not as outliers, and lower forecast-assessment confidence by one level (accuracy and verdict are not affected). When most months equal the median (median absolute deviation of zero) the outlier test cannot run, and the page says so. A production order counts as at least 1 day. Finished-goods safety stock assumes a fixed production time: production-time variability is not included, although raw-material safety stock does include supplier lead-time variability. The supplier score weights (50% on-time, 20% lead-time stability, 15% fill rate, 15% lateness) are fixed; only the class bands are adjustable.
