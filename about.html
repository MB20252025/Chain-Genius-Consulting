About Chain Genius Consulting

Practical insight. Better decisions.

We focus on making supply-chain information easier to understand and more useful in everyday business decisions.

Built around the realities of running a business.

Supply-chain decisions are connected. A purchasing issue can affect availability; a supplier delay can disrupt operations; and inventory decisions can have consequences for cash and business performance.

Chain Genius Consulting focuses on making those connections easier to understand. We combine practical operational thinking with analytical methods and purpose-built digital technology.

Our aim is to help businesses move beyond disconnected figures and towards clearer, evidence-supported decisions.

Practical thinkingFocus on real business questions and useful outcomes.
Explainable analysisMake findings understandable and traceable.
Connected intelligenceLook beyond isolated figures and departments.
Honest recommendationsMake assumptions, limitations and evidence clear.

Our approach

Technology supports expertise. It doesn't replace judgement.

Our Supply Chain Intelligence Engine is being developed to make analysis more consistent, repeatable and explainable. The technology supports the work; the business context, quality of information and interpretation remain essential.

We aim to be clear about what the data can tell us, what it cannot tell us and where further investigation is needed.
