What we do

Services designed around real operational challenges.

Practical analysis to help businesses understand inventory, suppliers, demand, purchasing, operations and working capital.

Our areas of focus

The scope of each engagement is agreed around your objectives, available data and the decisions you need to make.

01 / INVENTORY

Inventory & Stock Optimisation

Review stock positions, stock cover, excess and slow-moving inventory, availability and replenishment requirements.

02 / SUPPLIERS

Supplier Performance

Assess delivery performance, lead-time variation, purchasing history, supplier exposure and available reliability evidence.

03 / DEMAND

Demand & Forecasting

Explore historical demand, patterns, seasonality and suitable forecasting approaches where data supports them.

04 / PURCHASING

Purchasing & Replenishment

Examine purchasing requirements, order quantities, replenishment policies, minimum order constraints and material availability.

05 / OPERATIONS

Production & Operations Analysis

Explore production requirements, planning feasibility and operational constraints where the necessary information is available.

06 / FINANCIAL

Working Capital & Excess Stock

Understand inventory investment, excess stock exposure and the potential financial implications of operational decisions.

How engagements are scoped

We first establish the business question, the data available and the level of analysis that can be supported. We explain limitations rather than presenting unsupported conclusions.
